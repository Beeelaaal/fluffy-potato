# Pakistani Uni Admissions 2026: The Ultimate Deadlines Calendar (Save or Cry)

## Don't Get Left Behind, Yaar
Missing an admission deadline is the ultimate facepalm moment. To make sure you don't end up taking a gap year playing Valorant, bookmark this calendar of key entry test registration and test dates for Pakistani universities.

## 1. Engineering & Computing Timeline
- **NUST (NET Series)**:
 - NET-1: Registration (Oct-Nov), Test (Dec)
 - NET-2: Registration (Jan-Feb), Test (March)
 - NET-3: Registration (March-April), Test (May)
 - NET-4: Registration (May-June), Test (July) - *The final series!*
- **FAST-NUCES**:
 - Registration opens: Mid-May
 - Registration closes: Early July
 - Entry Test: Mid-July
- **GIKI**:
 - Registration closes: Early June
 - Entry Test: Mid-June

## 2. Business & Social Sciences Timeline
- **LUMS**:
 - Application Deadline: Early February
 - SAT/LCAT Deadline: Late February / March
- **IBA Karachi**:
 - Round 1 (Spring/Fall): Registration (Dec-Jan), Test (Feb)
 - Round 2 (Fall): Registration (April-May), Test (June)

## 3. Medical Timeline (MBBS/BDS)
- **MDCAT (National)**:
 - Registration opens: July
 - MDCAT Test Date: Late August or September (dates managed by PMDC)
- **AKU Entry Test**:
 - Registration closes: June
 - Entry Test: Mid-July

## Campus Life and Student Facilities

Pakistani universities offer a wide range of campus environments. From large residential campuses to modern urban centers, student life is about balancing studies with co-curriculars. The canteens, libraries, and common rooms are key spots where students gather, share notes, and build peer networks. Participating in university societies and sports tournaments helps you develop confidence, leadership skills, and a well-rounded personality, ensuring a memorable student life. Additionally, campuses provide digital libraries, research facilities, and career counseling services. Building a strong network with faculty, seniors, and alumni is essential to secure job placements and internships. University life is a transition stage that prepares you for professional careers, and staying active in both academics and co-curriculars will help you make the most of this journey.

## Grading System and CGPA Management

Grading systems vary between absolute and relative. Most universities align their grading policies with HEC guidelines. Maintaining a CGPA above 3.0 is highly beneficial for corporate jobs, internships, and higher education. Focus on consistent study, attend tutorials, and build practical skills during your degree. Ensure you understand the specific grading rules of your department to avoid any academic surprises. Additionally, track your grades after each term. If you face academic challenges, consult your course advisor for guidance on course selection or GPA recovery. Remember that a balanced approach, combining academic focus with professional skill development, will ensure long-term career success.

## Step-by-Step Guide and Strategic Tips

Never miss an admission deadline. Create a spreadsheet to track registration dates, fee deadlines, and exam schedules for different universities. Set reminders on your phone a week before each deadline. Gathering your documents early ensures you can submit your application before the portal closes. A missed deadline can result in a gap year, so stay organized. Verify the fee submission dates and lock in your seat promptly once your name appears on the merit list. Additionally, track the dates for document correction and list revisions. If your application has errors, the university will notify you on the dashboard, and you must resolve the issues within a short window. Review university notifications daily during the admission cycle and follow up with the admissions desk if you face payment verification issues.
