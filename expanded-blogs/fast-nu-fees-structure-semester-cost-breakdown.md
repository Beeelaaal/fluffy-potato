# FAST University Fees 2026: Cost Breakdown and Semester Expenses

## Counting the Cost
FAST-NU is a private sector university, which means its fees are higher than public institutions but significantly lower than premium universities like LUMS or GIKI. If you are planning to join FAST for CS, SE, or BBA, here is the detailed breakdown of the semester fees and other expenses.

## 1. Undergrad Tuition Fee Structure
FAST charges fees per credit hour. For the 2026 academic year, the tuition fee is calculated as follows:
- **Per Credit Hour Rate**: Approximately PKR 9,500.
- **Average Semester Load**: 15 to 18 credit hours.
- **Tuition Fee per Semester**: Ranges between PKR 150,000 and PKR 175,000 depending on the number of courses you enroll in.
- **Admission Fee (One-time)**: PKR 20,000 paid at the time of initial enrollment.
- **Security Deposit (Refundable)**: PKR 10,000.

## 2. Additional Semester Expenses
- **Hostel Fee**: FAST does not have hostels inside all campuses, but partner hostels nearby charge around PKR 15,000 to PKR 22,000 per month including mess food.
- **Books & Projects**: Around PKR 5,000 per semester. Most books are available digitally, so you will save money there.

## Campus Life and Student Facilities

FAST-NUCES campuses operate like fast-paced tech hubs. Whether you are at Lahore, Islamabad, or Karachi, the environment is highly focused on practical coding and software development. The canteens are packed with students sharing samosas and hot tea between programming slots, discussing complex logic designs, data structure assignments, and debugging tips. The libraries are filled with students working on project documentations, research papers, and group tasks. Since on-campus hostels are limited, most outstation students live in private hostels nearby, which requires managing private transport and meals independently, building real-world maturity. The student societies are active, organizing national-level computing events like SOFTEC and NaSCon, which connect you directly with tech recruiters and software houses. Campus life at FAST is a lesson in time management, preparing you directly for the corporate tech sector. Additionally, the labs are equipped with modern development tools, high-speed internet, and version control configurations, serving as the primary workplace for students. The faculty members are highly qualified, with deep ties to the local tech industry. During the final year, the campus hosts major recruitment drives where top software houses interview candidates, providing outstanding job placement rates. Students build strong peer connections, collaborating on coding projects, open-source contributions, and startup ideas, making the FAST experience highly practical and career-oriented.

## Grading System and CGPA Management

Grading at FAST is absolute. Your grade depends on your percentage score. Securing an A requires scoring above 85, regardless of the class average. This absolute system makes maintaining a high CGPA challenging, and a 2.5 CGPA from FAST is respected in the software industry. Focus on building coding skills, contributing to GitHub repositories, and managing the weekly lab evaluations. Do not lose hope if your first-semester GPA is lower than expected. Recruiters know the rigor of the curriculum, and your practical problem-solving skills will matter more than your GPA in job placements. Additionally, assignments have high weightage and strict plagiarism checks. Lab evaluations are conducted weekly, testing your ability to write and debug code within a limited time. To survive the GPA grind, build a strong group of classmates to discuss coding logic, and manage your time to avoid last-minute stress. Maintain focus on coding competency, as your technical portfolio will be the primary driver of your career placement.

## Step-by-Step Guide and Strategic Tips

Academic integrity is crucial at university. Never copy code or assignments, as universities use advanced plagiarism detection tools like Turnitin. Build a good relationship with your lab instructors and professors by being active in class and attending office hours. Stay active in university societies to build your network, team-working skills, and communication capabilities. Manage your time to balance your GPAs with your physical and mental health. Ask for help from seniors when you struggle with courses, and use the university counseling resources if you feel overwhelmed. Additionally, do not skip class lectures, as continuous evaluation contributes significantly to your grades. Do manage your daily commute and study hours, and do not compromise on building practical portfolio projects, as technical skills are the ultimate driver of career success.

## General Academic Excellence Guidelines

Pakistani universities offer a wide range of campus environments. From large residential campuses to modern urban centers, student life is about balancing studies with co-curriculars. The canteens, libraries, and common rooms are key spots where students gather, share notes, and build peer networks. Participating in university societies and sports tournaments helps you develop confidence, leadership skills, and a well-rounded personality, ensuring a memorable student life. Additionally, campuses provide digital libraries, research facilities, and career counseling services. Building a strong network with faculty, seniors, and alumni is essential to secure job placements and internships. University life is a transition stage that prepares you for professional careers, and staying active in both academics and co-curriculars will help you make the most of this journey.

Grading systems vary between absolute and relative. Most universities align their grading policies with HEC guidelines. Maintaining a CGPA above 3.0 is highly beneficial for corporate jobs, internships, and higher education. Focus on consistent study, attend tutorials, and build practical skills during your degree. Ensure you understand the specific grading rules of your department to avoid any academic surprises. Additionally, track your grades after each term. If you face academic challenges, consult your course advisor for guidance on course selection or GPA recovery. Remember that a balanced approach, combining academic focus with professional skill development, will ensure long-term career success.
