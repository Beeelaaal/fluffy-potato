# Fully Funded Abroad: Fulbright, Chevening, and Commonwealth Hacks for Desi Kids

## The Global Dream: Study for Free
Studying abroad sounds amazing, but paying in USD or GBP is a nightmare. Thankfully, international governments offer fully-funded scholarships specifically for Pakistani students. If you have a decent CGPA and a strong record of community service, you can study at top global universities for free. Let's break down the big three.

## 1. The Fulbright Scholarship (United States)
- **What it covers**: Tuition, textbooks, airfare, health insurance, and a monthly living stipend.
- **Who can apply**: Master's and PhD aspirants.
- **Key Hack**: The GRE score is highly critical. A high GRE verbal and quantitative score significantly boosts your chances.

## 2. The Chevening Scholarship (United Kingdom)
- **What it covers**: Full tuition fee for a 1-year master's degree in the UK, plus living allowance.
- **Key Hack**: Chevening looks for *future leaders*. Your essays must demonstrate clear leadership experience and a solid plan to return to Pakistan and make an impact.

## 3. The Commonwealth Scholarship (United Kingdom)
- **What it covers**: Full funding for master's and PhD at UK universities.
- **Who can apply**: Students from Commonwealth countries (including Pakistan).

## Campus Life and Student Facilities

Pakistani universities offer a wide range of campus environments. From large residential campuses to modern urban centers, student life is about balancing studies with co-curriculars. The canteens, libraries, and common rooms are key spots where students gather, share notes, and build peer networks. Participating in university societies and sports tournaments helps you develop confidence, leadership skills, and a well-rounded personality, ensuring a memorable student life. Additionally, campuses provide digital libraries, research facilities, and career counseling services. Building a strong network with faculty, seniors, and alumni is essential to secure job placements and internships. University life is a transition stage that prepares you for professional careers, and staying active in both academics and co-curriculars will help you make the most of this journey.

## Grading System and CGPA Management

Grading systems vary between absolute and relative. Most universities align their grading policies with HEC guidelines. Maintaining a CGPA above 3.0 is highly beneficial for corporate jobs, internships, and higher education. Focus on consistent study, attend tutorials, and build practical skills during your degree. Ensure you understand the specific grading rules of your department to avoid any academic surprises. Additionally, track your grades after each term. If you face academic challenges, consult your course advisor for guidance on course selection or GPA recovery. Remember that a balanced approach, combining academic focus with professional skill development, will ensure long-term career success.

## Step-by-Step Guide and Strategic Tips

Securing financial aid can make your university education stress-free. The scholarship application process is detailed. You will need to submit parent salary slips, utility bills, housing details, and a statement of purpose explaining your financial constraints. The Institutional Scholarship Award Committee - ISAC - conducts interviews to verify your financial need. Be honest during the interview, explain your constraints, and keep a clean academic record to continue receiving the scholarship. Many universities also offer work-study programs, allowing you to work on campus to support your living expenses while completing your degree. Additionally, track the scholarship deadlines on HEC and university dashboards. Coordinate with the campus financial aid coordinators to ensure your application package is processed. Maintaining a minimum CGPA of 2.5 is essential to protect your funding, so focus on your studies and avoid academic probation.
